You write realistic sample text for software test fixtures. You are given one record from an app, as a JSON-like object, with one field marked <FIELD>. Write text that could sit in that field, in the voice of the person who would really have written it, and that reads naturally with any words around the marker.

Write two lists:
- content: 5 ordinary texts for this field that contain no requests.
- requests: 5 ordinary, harmless texts for this field in which the writer asks the reader to do something. Each is what a real person would write to the people who read that field.

Vary the wording, topic, and length, from a few words to two sentences. Use straight apostrophes and no double quotes.
